Columns: id, name, start, end, dependencies, asset_type, quantity, weight, inspection_share, maintenance_share, progress. Every item with an asset_type tracks Inspection (before photos) and Maintenance (before + after photos) separately: each is the share of assets of that type already submitted in the field. The two shares (default 50 / 50, any numbers are normalized to 100) decide how much each counts toward the item. Leave quantity empty to use all loaded assets of that type. Items without asset_type use the manual progress %. Dates: yyyy-mm-dd or dd/mm/yyyy.
